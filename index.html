Weatheria

Weatheria is a free weather forecast site covering over 5 million places worldwide. Besides hourly and 7-day forecasts, it highlights the best two-hour window each day to be outside, and lets you compare up to three cities side by side.

  • 🌍 11 languages, no sign-up needed
  • ⏱️ Best outdoor window each day
  • 🏙️ Compare up to three cities
